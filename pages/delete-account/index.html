Account & Data Deletion

Permanent account and workspace erasure.

Users of Ledgo: Freelance Ledger (com.dfile.dfile_mobile), published by Yassine Grib, can permanently erase their account and all synced cloud records either directly inside the Android app or from the web on this page.

Application: Ledgo: Freelance Ledger Developer: Yassine Grib Retention After Erasure: 0 Days
Method A · Instant

Delete directly inside the Ledgo app

Recommended if you still have Ledgo installed on your phone. Erasure runs immediately against Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore:

  1. Open Ledgo and sign in to your workspace.
  2. Open Settings and tap Privacy and data.
  3. Optional: tap Export my data to save a JSON backup first.
  4. Under Danger Zone, tap Delete account, type DELETE, confirm your credentials, and tap Delete my account.
Method B · Web Request

Request deletion without the app

If you already uninstalled Ledgo, submit a request from the email address registered to your account. Requests are completed within 7 calendar days.

Your email client has been opened with a pre-addressed request to gribyassine@gmail.com. Send the email from your registered address to finalize verification.

What Is Deleted

Both deletion methods permanently purge every record linked to your user UID:

  • Profile & Authentication: Your Firebase Authentication record (email/password or Google Sign-In link), name, phone number, and business address.
  • Clients & Projects: All client profiles, project budgets, deadlines, calendar milestones, and task checklists.
  • Financial Ledger: All payment records, receipts, expense entries, and friends' debt statements.
  • Settings & Support Logs: Workspace preferences and any open support tickets tied to your UID.
Retention Policy

No copies are retained after deletion. In-app deletion erases your Firestore documents and authentication account immediately during the confirmation session. Web email requests sent to gribyassine@gmail.com are verified against the sender address and permanently erased within 7 days, followed by a written confirmation reply.
