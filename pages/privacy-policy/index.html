Privacy Policy

Your ledger data stays under your ownership.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ledgo: Freelance Ledger (com.dfile.dfile_mobile), developed and published by Yassine Grib, collects, how it is stored, and how you control or erase it.

Effective Date: October 31, 2026 Package ID: com.dfile.dfile_mobile Contact: gribyassine@gmail.com
01 / Summary

Ledgo is a bookkeeping and project tracking application for independent freelancers. We collect only the account credentials and business records you explicitly enter so your workspace can synchronize across your signed-in devices.

Never Sold Encrypted in Transit No Advertising SDKs User Deletable
02 / Data Collected

When you register and use Ledgo, the application processes the following categories of user-provided data:

Category Specific Fields Purpose
Account & Profile Full name, email address, optional phone number, optional business address, authentication identifier (Email/Password or Google Sign-In). Account authentication, cloud workspace synchronization, and displaying your sender details on client statements.
Clients & Projects Client names, contact details, project titles, categories, budgets, deadlines, milestones, and task checklists. Core project management and deadline calendar views inside your private workspace.
Ledger Entries Recorded payment amounts, dates, payment method labels (BaridiMob, CCP, bank transfer, cash), work expenses, and friends' debt balances. Calculating net profit, outstanding client balances, collection rates, and personal debt statements.
App Preferences & Support Language, theme preference, local privacy PIN state, notification preferences, and voluntary support ticket messages. Preserving your interface configuration and responding to bug reports you submit.
03 / Financial Clarification

Ledgo does not connect to bank APIs, process payments, hold funds, issue credit, or read SMS messages. All monetary figures in the app are manual ledger entries created by the user.

04 / Storage & Security

Local preferences are stored on your Android device, and synchronized workspace records are hosted on Google Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore.

  • All network communication between the Android client and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firestore security rules scope every document to your authenticated user UID so no other user can read or modify your records.
  • Ledgo includes no third-party advertising networks, behavioral trackers, or cross-app profiling SDKs, and does not access the Android Advertising ID.
05 / Data Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data. Data is processed solely by Google Firebase (Google LLC) acting as our cloud infrastructure provider for authentication and database hosting, or when you explicitly tap the WhatsApp reminder button to open a pre-addressed message on your own device.

06 / Export & Deletion

You retain full control over your records at all times:

  • Full JSON Export: Navigate to Settings → Privacy and data → Export my data inside the app to export a machine-readable JSON copy of your entire ledger.
  • In-App Instant Deletion: Navigate to Settings → Privacy and data → Delete account to permanently erase your Firebase Auth account and all Firestore collections immediately.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you no longer have the app installed, submit a deletion request on our Account & Data Deletion page.
07 / Children's Privacy

Ledgo is designed for adult professionals and freelancers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).
